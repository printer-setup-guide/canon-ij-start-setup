Common Canon Printer Setup Errors

  • Error code 5100 or 1401: Cartridge carriage blocked or unrecognized
  • Canon printer not responding over Wi-Fi network
  • Yellow alarm light blinking multiple times
  • IJ Start Canon download stalled or failed
  • Support Code 1003: No paper in rear tray
  • Canon PIXMA wireless connect mode timeout
  • MegaTank Issues: Air trapped in Canon MegaTank ink tubes
  • Canon Scanner driver not found by operating system

Canon Setup Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my Canon PIXMA to Wi-Fi?

Press and hold the Wireless Connect button on your Canon printer for 3 seconds. Then run Canon setup on your computer or smartphone to automatically transfer network credentials.

What does a flashing orange light on Canon printer mean?

The number of orange blinks indicates specific codes (e.g., 2 blinks = out of paper, 4 blinks = ink cartridge not installed properly). Check your support manual or contact chat support.

Where can I download Canon Printer drivers?

Get official Canon drivers via our support assistant or Canon official support setup portal.
